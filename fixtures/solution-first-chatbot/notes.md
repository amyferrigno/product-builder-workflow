# Leadership Sync - Oct 3, 2026

## Attendees
VP Product, PM, Engineer Lead

---

VP Product: I've been thinking about where we compete. Our scheduling tool is table stakes, but customer support is expensive. I want us to build an AI chatbot to handle customer support automatically.

PM: What kind of questions would the chatbot answer?

VP Product: Support questions. You know, how to reschedule a meeting, what calendar systems we support, that kind of thing. Our support team spends all day on repetitive questions.

Engineer Lead: Have you talked to support about what they're spending time on?

VP Product: Not yet. But every SaaS company has a chatbot now. It's table stakes. We should have one too.

PM: I can check with support on their top issues. But before we spec this, do you have a sense of the data we'd need to train something like this?

VP Product: Well, we have support tickets. That should be plenty.

Engineer Lead: How many tickets per question type?

VP Product: I don't know the exact breakdown. Maybe a few hundred per month?

Engineer Lead: That's pretty thin if we want to handle diverse questions reliably.

VP Product: Look, competition is shipping this. We need to keep up. Let's just build it.

PM: Let me gather some data first. I'll get the support ticket breakdown and see if there's actual demand from customers.

VP Product: Okay, but move fast. I want a prototype by end of Q4.

PM: That might be aggressive if we need to collect data first. Can we revisit in two weeks once I have numbers?

VP Product: Sure. Just don't let this slow down. Chatbots are the future.
