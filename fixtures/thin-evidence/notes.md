# Team Feedback Session - Oct 3, 2026

## Attendees
Product Lead, Designer, Engineer Lead

---

Designer: I've been using our app every day and I think the meeting creation flow is confusing. Too many steps.

Product Lead: How confusing?

Designer: Like, when I create a meeting, I have to click through title, description, attendees, time, and then confirm. That's five steps. Most of the time I just want to specify the attendees and time.

Product Lead: Are customers complaining about it?

Designer: I haven't formally asked, but it just feels slow. I think we should streamline it to two steps: quick mode for just attendees and time, or full mode for all the details.

Engineer Lead: How much faster would that be?

Designer: I don't know, maybe a few seconds. It's more about the mental load of clicking through a dialog.

Product Lead: Have you tested this with real users?

Designer: No, this is just my observation using the product daily. But I'm pretty confident it's an issue.

Engineer Lead: It might be a real issue, but we're kind of guessing. We could add tracking to see how many users actually change the attendees or description after creating a meeting. That would tell us if anyone cares about the order.

Product Lead: Good idea. Let's gather some data before we redesign.

Designer: Sure, but I'm pretty sure users feel the same way.

Product Lead: We'll find out. Let me add some analytics this sprint and we can revisit next month.
