## 1. Decision

No PRD yet for the support chatbot. The notes hold an opinion and a guess, not evidence, so the next move is to gather data, not to write a spec (D-1, D-2).

## 2. Problem hypothesis

PB-1: support may be spending too much effort on repetitive questions. It is unvalidated. The VP Product says support is expensive with no figure given (P-1) and that the support team answers repetitive questions all day, which has not been checked with support (P-2, Q-3). The notes started from a solution, not a problem (S-1).

## 3. Why not now

- The only volume figure is a guess of maybe a few hundred tickets, and there is no breakdown per question type (M-1, Q-1).
- The Engineer Lead called that amount thin for a chatbot that must handle diverse questions reliably (M-2).
- Nobody has confirmed customer demand (Q-2) or what support really spends its time on (Q-3).
- The AI gate result in the problem file is needs data, so AI is not justified yet: most gates cannot be answered from the notes. The reason to act is that competitors ship chatbots (R-3), which is not evidence of a problem here.

## 4. What would change this

- Ticket counts by question type, enough to show whether repetitive questions dominate. Owner: PM. Cheapest way: export the ticket breakdown the PM already agreed to get (A-2, Q-1).
- Confirmation from the support team of what they spend their time on. Owner: PM. Cheapest way: a short conversation with support about their top issues (A-1, Q-3).
- Signs of real customer demand for automated support. Owner: PM. Cheapest way: look at the same ticket data for customers asking for faster answers (Q-2).
- A cost of support figure, so the return can be judged. Owner: VP Product. Cheapest way: ask whoever tracks support spend for the existing figure (P-1).

## 5. Cheapest next step

PM gets the support ticket breakdown and checks with support on their top issues (A-1, A-2), then the group revisits in two weeks (D-2).

## 6. Parked solution idea

The VP Product proposed an AI chatbot to handle customer support automatically (S-1), for questions such as how to reschedule a meeting and which calendar systems are supported (R-1). It is parked, not endorsed. A prototype by end of Q4 was requested (R-2); that timing is not supported by any evidence yet.
